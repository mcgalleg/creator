# AI Copilot

The AI Copilot is your intelligent analytics assistant. Ask questions about your TikTok content in plain English and get data-driven answers instantly.

## How It Works

The AI Copilot uses your synced TikTok data to answer questions. When you ask a question, the copilot:

1. Analyzes your query to determine what data is needed
2. Queries your synced TikTok data (videos, comments, engagement metrics)
3. Returns a clear, actionable response with relevant data

## What You Can Ask

### Content Performance

- "What are my top 10 videos by views?"
- "Which videos got the most likes this month?"
- "Show me my worst performing videos"

### Engagement Analysis

- "What's my average engagement rate?"
- "Which videos have the highest comment-to-view ratio?"
- "How has my engagement changed over the past 30 days?"

### Comment Insights

- "What's the overall sentiment of my comments?"
- "Show me my most replied-to videos"
- "Are there any negative comments I should address?"

### Trends & Patterns

- "What time of day do my videos perform best?"
- "What content topics get the most engagement?"
- "Compare my performance this month vs last month"

## Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific** — "Top 5 videos by views this week" is better than "show me videos"
- **Specify time ranges** — "Last 7 days" or "this month" helps narrow results
- **Ask follow-up questions** — The copilot remembers your conversation context
- **Request different formats** — Ask for tables, summaries, or detailed breakdowns

## AI Token Usage

Each AI Copilot interaction consumes AI tokens based on the complexity of your query and the response length. Your current token balance is displayed in the workspace header. See [AI Tokens](/docs/ai-tokens) for details on allocation and limits.
