# AI Tokens

AI tokens power the AI Copilot. Each query and response consumes tokens based on the complexity and length of the interaction.

## How Tokens Work

- **Monthly allocation** — Each subscription tier includes a monthly token allocation
- **Usage per query** — Tokens are consumed for both the input (your question) and the output (the AI response)
- **No rollover** — Unused monthly tokens do not carry over

## Monthly Allocations by Tier

| Tier | Monthly AI Tokens |
|------|------------------|
| Free | 100,000 |
| Creator | 1,000,000 |
| Pro | 3,000,000 |
| Agency | 10,000,000 |
| MCP Apps | Bring your own AI |

## MCP Apps Tier

The MCP Apps tier does not include AI tokens — instead, you connect your own AI client (such as Claude Desktop) and use your own API keys. This gives you unlimited AI usage at your own cost.

## AI Token Packs

Need more tokens? Purchase one-time token packs from the [pricing page](/pricing#ai-tokens).

## Checking Your Balance

Your current token balance is displayed in the workspace header with a sparkles icon. The badge turns amber when you're running low.

## Tips for Managing Tokens

- **Be concise** — Shorter, focused questions use fewer tokens
- **Use specific queries** — Targeted questions produce shorter, more efficient responses
- **Check your balance** regularly in the workspace header
