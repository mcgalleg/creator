# Sync Credits

Sync credits are used to pull fresh data from TikTok into your Astriq dashboard. Each sync operation consumes credits based on the amount of data being fetched.

## How Credits Work

- **Monthly allocation** — Each subscription tier includes a monthly credit allocation that refreshes at the start of each billing cycle
- **One credit per sync unit** — Credits are consumed when syncing videos, comments, and engagement data
- **No rollover** — Unused monthly credits do not carry over to the next month

## Monthly Allocations by Tier

| Tier | Monthly Credits |
|------|----------------|
| Free | 50 |
| Creator | 500 |
| Pro | 1,500 |
| Agency | 4,000 |
| MCP Apps | Pay-as-you-go |

## Credit Packs

Need more credits? Purchase one-time credit packs that **never expire**:

- **Starter** — 100 credits for $4.99
- **Value** — 300 credits for $9.99
- **Power** — 750 credits for $19.99
- **Bulk** — 1,500 credits for $34.99

Credit packs are used after your monthly allocation is exhausted.

## Checking Your Balance

Your current credit balance is always visible in the workspace header. The badge turns amber when you're running low (below 10% of your tier's allocation).

Click the credit badge to go directly to the [pricing page](/pricing#credits) to purchase more.

## Tips for Managing Credits

- **Sync strategically** — Focus syncs on your most active accounts
- **Use credit packs** for heavy sync periods without upgrading your plan
- **Monitor usage** via the credit badge in the workspace header
