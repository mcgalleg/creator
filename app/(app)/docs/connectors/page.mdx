# Connectors

Connectors extend Astriq's capabilities by integrating external tools and services through the MCP (Model Context Protocol) standard.

## What Are Connectors?

Connectors are MCP-compatible tools that add new abilities to your AI Copilot. When enabled, the copilot can use these tools to perform additional actions beyond standard TikTok analytics.

## Browsing Connectors

1. Navigate to **Workspace > Connectors** from the workspace
2. Browse the connector directory
3. Click on any connector to see its description and capabilities
4. Toggle connectors on or off as needed

## How Connectors Work

When you enable a connector, the AI Copilot gains access to that connector's tools. For example, a visualization connector might let the copilot generate charts and diagrams in response to your queries.

Connectors run within a secure sandbox environment and only have access to the data you explicitly provide through your queries.

## MCP Hub

If you're on the **MCP Apps** tier, you can also use the **MCP Hub** to connect external AI clients (like Claude Desktop) directly to your Astriq data. This lets you use your preferred AI tools with your TikTok analytics.

Navigate to **Workspace > MCP Hub** to get your connection details and setup instructions.
