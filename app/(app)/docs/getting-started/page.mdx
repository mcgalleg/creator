# Getting Started

Get up and running with Astriq in just a few minutes.

## 1. Create Your Account

Sign up for a free Astriq account. No credit card required — the Free tier gives you 50 sync credits, 100K AI tokens, and 1 connected TikTok account to start.

## 2. Connect Your TikTok Account

After signing in, you'll be guided through the onboarding flow to connect your first TikTok account. Astriq uses the official TikTok API to access your data securely.

1. Click **Connect TikTok Account** on the onboarding screen
2. Authorize Astriq to access your TikTok data
3. Wait for the initial sync to complete — this usually takes a minute or two

## 3. Explore Your Workspace

Once your data is synced, you'll land in the **Workspace** — your AI-powered analytics hub. From here you can:

- **Ask the AI Copilot** questions about your content performance
- **View your accounts** and trigger manual syncs
- **Browse connectors** to extend functionality

## 4. Ask Your First Question

Try typing a question in the AI Copilot chat, such as:

- "What are my top performing videos this week?"
- "Which videos have the most comments?"
- "Show me engagement trends for the last 30 days"

The AI Copilot will query your synced TikTok data and return actionable insights.
