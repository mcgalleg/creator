# Support

Have a question or running into an issue? We're here to help.

## Contact Us

Reach out to our support team at [support@astriq.ai](mailto:support@astriq.ai). We typically respond within 24 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

For quick answers to common questions, visit the [FAQ section](/#faq) on our homepage.

## Report a Bug

If you've found a bug, please email us at [support@astriq.ai](mailto:support@astriq.ai) with:

- A description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- Screenshots if applicable
- Your browser and device information
